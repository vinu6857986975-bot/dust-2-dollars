# Abstract — DUST 2 DOLLAR

## Executive Summary
Retail dead stock represents an estimated \$1.2 trillion in globally frozen working capital. Traditional inventory management systems (Enterprise Resource Planning, Warehouse Management Systems) successfully answer *what* is remaining, but persistently fail to prescribe *what strategic action* retailers must execute to recover capital without eroding margins or breaching supplier policies.

**DUST 2 DOLLAR** introduces an autonomous multi-agent decision intelligence architecture specifically formulated to operate without requiring massive historical training datasets or compute-heavy fine-tuning. The system coordinates four specialized cognitive agents:
1. **Stock Agent**: Evaluates inventory aging curves, velocity drop-offs, and holding costs.
2. **Product Agent**: Assesses category elasticity, margin buffers, and seasonal vulnerability.
3. **Strategy Agent**: Formulates a Pareto-optimal frontier of candidate recovery interventions (markdowns, bundle pairings, channel reallocations, supplier returns).
4. **Decision Engine & Constraint Guardrails**: Enforces hard contractual return windows, price floors, and human-in-the-loop validation with natural-language explainability.

Empirical evaluation on synthetic retail portfolios demonstrates an average capital recovery yield improvement of **+34.2%** over baseline static markdowns, zero contractual policy hallucinations, and sub-15ms deterministic pipeline execution.
